Privacy Policy — FINANCIAL TIMES Publisher

Last updated: October 18, 2026

FINANCIAL TIMES Publisher ("the App") is a content publishing tool operated by the FINANCIAL TIMES finance brand. This policy explains what data the App accesses through TikTok's APIs and how it is handled.

1. Data we access

When you authorize the App with your TikTok account, the App receives an OAuth access token with only the scopes you approve. The App requests the minimum scopes needed to publish your own videos: video.upload and video.publish (to upload and publish videos you created), and user.info.basic (to confirm which account authorized the App). The App does not read your private messages, contacts, or other users' data.

2. How we use your data

Your TikTok data is used solely to upload and publish videos to your own TikTok account, on your instruction. We do not sell, rent, or share your TikTok data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only to perform publishing actions you requested. Tokens are refreshed automatically as needed and can be revoked at any time from your TikTok settings (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions), which immediately stops the App's access.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep only the minimum records needed to operate the publishing queue (e.g., which videos were posted and when). If you revoke access or ask us to delete your data, we delete stored tokens and publishing records promptly.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above and note the change where the App is administered.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy or to request deletion of your data, contact the FINANCIAL TIMES team through the blog at financialtimesofficial.blogspot.com.
